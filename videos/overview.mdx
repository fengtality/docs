## Agent Builders Cup

The **Agent Builders Cup** is a one-month online hackathon where builders compete for a share of the **$20K prize pool** by deploying AI trading agents that trade real capital on live markets. Each sponsor venue got its own full-length workshop covering how to build a winning agent there.

## The Bot Pod

<Frame>
  <img src="/videos/botpod-new-cover.png" alt="The Bot Pod" />

**The Bot Pod** is a weekly live show from Hummingbot where maintainers Mike Feng and Federico Cardoso demo the latest features in Condor and Hummingbot, dive deep into trading strategies, and build live on stream. New episodes stream every **Friday at 8:00 AM Pacific / 11:00 AM Eastern**.
